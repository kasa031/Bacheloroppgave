Problem

Phishing remains the dominant entry point to serious cyber incidents, yet technical controls and awareness campaigns have not eliminated it. In Norway, public skepticism toward unknown senders has increased, while loneliness is elevated among young adults and students (Statistics Norway, 2025; Khrono & Norwegian Student Organisation, 2024).

Research problem: Why does phishing remain effective when people are both more lonely and more skeptical, and which emotional and contextual factors make individuals in higher education especially vulnerable? The thesis introduces the skepticism paradox as its analytical lens.

Research question

Main RQ: How can phishing remain effective in a context of increased loneliness and increased general skepticism, and which emotional, cognitive, and contextual factors make individuals especially vulnerable to social engineering?

Hypotheses: H1 loneliness and social lures; H2 skepticism × lure type interaction; H3 situational mediation; H4 protective factors (PMT).
