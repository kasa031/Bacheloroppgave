ACIT4280 Privacy by Design - group submission

Internal overview for the group. Deadline 3 September 2026, 08:00. 40 points.

The submission has two parts. Part 1 is a Webbkoll analysis of 18 Norwegian-facing sites (table and graphics per sector). Part 2 is an ICO lawful basis assessment of five services. Deliverables: an English report (1000-1500 words plus diagrams) and a 20-minute presentation.

1. The two parts of the assignment

Part 1 - Webbkoll

  • 18 services: shopping, government, news/media, sport
  • Per site: server country, internal cookies, external cookies, third-party requests, countries outside Norway
  • Graphics per sector
  • Highest and lowest values, plus deviations

Part 2 - ICO lawful basis

  • Five services from the Webbkoll list
  • Find the privacy notice
  • Run the ICO tool on one specific purpose
  • Compare the notice with the ICO outcome
  • Summarise in the report (Table 5)

Cookies, membership, checkout and advertising are different purposes. Each ICO run covers one purpose.

3. Part 1 - Webbkoll

Method: English Webbkoll, one Chromium visit, no add-ons, Do Not Track off. Server country = KeyCDN country. Norway is dropped from the country list. Table 2 is the first measurement. Table 3 is a repeat measurement and does not replace Table 2.

  • Highest requests (Table 2): fotball.no 113, worldofwarcraft.com 90, document.no 51, aftenposten.no 48, klassekampen.no 46.
  • Lowest: lanekassen.no 1, altinn.no 5, nrk.no 7, spotify.no 10, skiforeningen.no 14.
  • Only ikea.no had third-party cookies (2), after redirect to ikea.com.
  • fotball.no has the most requests and still zero external cookies. Request volume is not cookie volume.
  • document.no has the widest first country list (6 countries excluding Norway).
  • Sector: news/media highest, government lowest, shopping in between. babyshop.no rises to 101 on the repeat measurement; ranking stays on the first measurement (45).
  • Method example: klassekampen.no, 20 Aug 2026 12:36:46 UTC: 4 first-party cookies, 0 third-party cookies, 56 requests.

Webbkoll does not read the privacy notice or decide Article 6. That is Part 2.

Main findings in Part 2

  1. Four of five core purposes match the notice: law for tax, contract for Netflix and Babyshop checkout, legitimate interests for NFF match history.
  2. Two consent tests are Partial: Skatteetaten cookies (the request does not fully name controller, purpose and type) and document.no Google Signals (the request is not separate from the terms).
