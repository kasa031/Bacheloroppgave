ACIT4280 Group 1A - ICO lawful basis worksheet

Selected services: skatteetaten.no, netflix.no, fotball.no, document.no and babyshop.no.

Law under Article 6 is not cookie consent

Skatteetaten processes tax and registry data under law. Optional web cookies on skatteetaten.no are offered through a Ja/Nei banner. Run the ICO tool on one purpose at a time. Do not treat a cookie click as the lawful basis for every other activity.

Cookie consent test (Nei)

Webbkoll does not click banners. For skatteetaten.no open skatteetaten.no/person/ in a clean browser, choose Nei, then record cookies and third-party requests. Save the banner screenshot as figures/fig7_skatteetaten_cookie_banner.png.

One purpose at a time

A service does not have one lawful basis for everything it does. State one concrete processing activity before using the ICO tool. Record what the privacy notice claims, then check whether the ICO result supports it.

Open the ICO lawful basis tool ICO guide to lawful basis

Repeat this process for each service

  1. Open the privacy notice and find a specific processing purpose.
  2. Write the purpose and the personal data used.
  3. Write the lawful basis claimed by the notice, if stated.
  4. Run the ICO tool for that purpose.
  5. Save the ICO result and explain whether it matches the notice.
  6. Do not treat a cookie choice as the lawful basis for every other activity.

ICO tool - suggested answers (tax and registry)

State the purpose first: processing identity, income and tax data to administer tax and maintain the population register. Start with No on contract.

  1. Contract Q1: Do you have (or intend to have) a contract with the individual? No - statutory tax and registry duties are not a commercial contract.
  2. Click Continue. Q2 and Q3 are skipped when Q1 is No.
  3. Consent: Can you offer a genuine choice about this processing? No - core tax and registry processing is required by law and cannot be refused.
  4. Consent: Is consent a precondition of the service? No
  5. Consent: Can the individual refuse without detriment? No
  6. Answer No (or I don't know only if unsure) on remaining consent checks, then click Continue.
  7. Legal obligation: Are you processing to comply with a legal obligation? Yes - tax acts and folkeregister rules require this processing.
  8. Legal obligation: Is the processing necessary for that compliance? Yes
  9. Legal obligation: Do you have discretion about how you comply? No
  10. Legal obligation: Could you reasonably comply without this data? No
  11. Public task: official tasks or functions in the public interest? Yes. Basis in law? Yes. Could you reasonably perform the task without this data? No.

Public task vs legal obligation: Choose legal obligation when a named statute requires this exact processing and the person has no real choice (for example reporting income or maintaining folkeregister entries under specific acts). Choose public task when Skatteetaten performs an official government function with clear basis in law, but you describe the broader public-interest task rather than one named duty. For tax administration both can fit. Emphasise named statutory duties if the tool points to legal obligation; emphasise official authority and public-interest functions if it points to public task.

ICO result: legal obligation and public task APPROPRIATE. Consent NOT APPROPRIATE / likely invalid. Contract and LI rejected. Inconclusive section empty.

Separate ICO run. Purpose: setting optional web statistics cookies after the Ja/Nei banner on skatteetaten.no. Start with No on contract.

ICO tool - suggested answers (player publication)

Purpose: publishing active player name and club (age 13+) on fotball.no to show match history. Start with No on contract.

  1. Contract Q1: Do you have a contract for this publication purpose? No - showing match history online is not the same as performing a membership contract.
  2. Click Continue.
  3. Consent: Can you offer a genuine choice about this publication? No - match history is published by default; club opt-out is not the same as Article 6 consent for this purpose.
  4. Answer No on further consent checks (precondition, refuse without detriment, and so on), then click Continue.
  5. Legal obligation: Processing to comply with a legal obligation? No
  6. Vital interests: Processing to protect vital interests? No
  7. Public task: Are you a public authority performing an official task? No - NFF is a sports federation, not a UK-style public authority.
  8. Legitimate interests: Are you processing to further legitimate interests? Yes - transparent match history for sport and fans.
  9. Legitimate interests: Is the processing necessary? Yes
  10. Legitimate interests: Have you carried out a balancing test (and is opt-out available)? Yes - NFF describes public-interest match history and club opt-out.
