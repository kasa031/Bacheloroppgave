Webbkoll and ICO findings

Karina Sætersdal Nilssen · ACIT4280 Privacy by Design · due 3 September 2026

This briefing records the measurements and lawful-basis results I produced for the group assignment. Part A is Webbkoll. Part B is the ICO tool. The two parts answer different questions and are not mixed.

A. Webbkoll findings

Tool: English Webbkoll. One live Chromium visit, no add-ons, Do Not Track off. Server country is the KeyCDN Country field. Norway is excluded from the country count. Table 2 is the first measurement on 20 August 2026. Table 3 is the repeat measurement.

Webbkoll reports HTTPS, cookies, third-party requests and the server IP. It does not read a privacy notice and it does not decide Article 6.

A3. Pattern from the Webbkoll set

  • Highest request counts: fotball.no 113, worldofwarcraft.com 90, document.no 51, aftenposten.no 48, klassekampen.no 46.
  • Lowest request counts: lanekassen.no 1, altinn.no 5, nrk.no 7, spotify.no 10, skiforeningen.no 14.

  • document.no has the widest first country list (6, excluding Norway).
  • By sector, news and media contact the most hosts. Government contacts the fewest. Shopping sits between those extremes.
  • On the repeat clock, babyshop.no rises from 45 to 101 requests and klassekampen.no from 46 to 56. Rankings still follow the first measurement.

B. ICO findings

Tool: ICO Lawful basis interactive guidance (updated 14 April 2026). Official Word reports dated 26 August 2026. One concrete purpose per run. Recognised legitimate interest fitted none of these purposes.

Each ICO run covers one purpose. Mixing tax data and optional statistics cookies in one run returns the wrong basis.

B1. skatteetaten.no: tax and the National Population Register

  • Purpose: identity, income and tax data for tax administration and the National Population Register.
  • Not included: Skyra, Matomo, Google Analytics, Siteimprove.
  • Notice: processing is required by law. Opt-out is generally not possible. Controller: the Director General of Taxation.
  • ICO result: legal obligation APPROPRIATE; public task APPROPRIATE; consent NOT APPROPRIATE / likely invalid; other bases NOT APPROPRIATE.
  • Match: Yes
  • Report: Skatteetaten_Lawful basis assessment report.docx

B2. skatteetaten.no: optional statistics cookies

  • Purpose: optional statistics cookies named in the notice (Skyra, Matomo, Google Analytics, Siteimprove).
  • Not included: tax and registry processing.
  • Notice: the cookie page claims consent for optional statistics tools.
  • ICO result: consent INCONCLUSIVE. The request does not fully name controller, purpose and types. No basis APPROPRIATE.
  • Match: Partial. The notice aims at consent. The ICO report says the request is too thin.
  • Report: Skatteetaten_cookies_Lawful basis assessment report.docx

Tax data and optional statistics cookies are two purposes and two ICO reports.

B3. netflix.no: paid streaming

  • Purpose: account and payment data to provide the paid subscription.
  • Not included: recommendations, advertising, marketing.
  • Notice (EEA/UK): contractual necessity to provide the service to members. Last updated 10 April 2026.
  • ICO result: contract APPROPRIATE; consent NOT APPROPRIATE / likely invalid (no genuine ongoing choice); other bases NOT APPROPRIATE.
  • Match: Yes
  • Report: Netflix_Lawful basis assessment report.docx

B5. ICO summary

  • Four of five core purposes match the notice: law for tax, contract for Netflix and Babyshop checkout, legitimate interests for NFF match history.
  • Two consent tests fail: optional cookies on skatteetaten.no, and Google Signals on document.no.
  • Optional cookies, a paid subscription, match history and checkout are separate ICO purposes.
